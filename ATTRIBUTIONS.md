# Vocabulary attribution

The 800-word JLPT N5 study curriculum is derived from the first 800 entries of
[9elt/jlpt-n5-word-list](https://github.com/9elt/jlpt-n5-word-list), used under
the MIT License.

Common kanji spellings and usually-kana guidance were audited against
[JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
which is maintained by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development
Group (EDRDG) and distributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 License](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html).
The spelling overrides and generated vocabulary data are distributed under the
same license.

The Japanese-Language Proficiency Test does not publish an official vocabulary,
kanji, or grammar checklist for the current test. “N5 vocabulary” therefore
means a curated study list rather than an official required-word specification.
